H2 Is It Legal to Download Facebook Videos?

H3 What Facebook's Terms Say

Facebook's Terms of Service state that content belongs to the original creator. Downloading videos for personal, offline use — such as saving a video of your own post, a public event, or educational content to watch without internet — is generally considered acceptable in most jurisdictions. What is not acceptable is downloading someone else's video and re-uploading it, monetizing it, or distributing it without permission. Always respect the original creator's rights.

H3 Safe and Responsible Use Cases

✅ Acceptable Use of Facebook Video Downloads

  • Downloading videos to repurpose with the creator's explicit permission

H2 Common Problems When Downloading Facebook Videos in HD — Solved

H3 Why Is the Video Downloading in Low Quality?

This happens when the original video was uploaded in SD or when the downloader defaults to a lower resolution. Always manually select the highest available quality option (720p, 1080p, 2K, or 4K) after pasting the URL. If only SD is available, the original upload was in low resolution — the downloader cannot improve upon the source file.

H3 Why Is the Video Downloading Without Sound?

Some high-resolution Facebook videos (especially 4K) have video and audio stored in separate streams on Facebook's servers. Advanced downloader tools automatically merge the video and audio tracks before delivering the final MP4 file. If your downloaded video has no sound, try selecting a slightly lower resolution (e.g., 1080p instead of 4K), which typically comes pre-merged.

H3 Why Won't the Downloader Detect My Facebook Video URL?

The most common causes are: using a shortened fb.watch link (try the full URL), the video being private or restricted, or the video being a Story that has already expired. Copy the full post URL directly from your browser's address bar when on a desktop for the most reliable results.
